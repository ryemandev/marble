## Old masters, 3 November 2026 (public domain and CC0)
The flex cards' "Old masters" shelf, picked by the owner from a sheet of candidates. Each is a public-domain painting, print or drawing, from the museum's or Wikimedia Commons' own scan; each licence was checked again when it was downloaded. The originals here are cut to at most 4000 across, and five are cropped: the frame off La Tour's Magdalen, the signatures off Doré's Pale Horse, the plate margin off Goya's Colossus, and the paper margins off both Bellows lithographs and Yoshitoshi's triptych.
