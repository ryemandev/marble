## Rules for anything posted from this kit
- Never promise or imply profits. Never write "rat race", "financial freedom", "quit your job", "get funded" or "pass your evaluation".
- Say "ink" in public, never "liquid".
- Any example number is labelled as sample data.
- A trader's card is never used in marketing unless a solicitor has cleared it.
- Firm logos stay off posts until the rights are checked.
- Don't claim journaling improves results.
- No statues in any picture. Old masters, anime and Japan only.
- The free line is the owner's: free forever, £0, every feature.

Rebuild these images with `node .scratch/social.cjs` from `app/` while the dev server runs.
