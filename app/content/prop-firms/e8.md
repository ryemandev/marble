## What stands out

**E8 Pro has a static drawdown.** On the 50K: a $4,000 target, a $1,250 daily loss limit that ends the account and $4,000 of fixed drawdown, with up to 50 lots.

**E8 One trails on your closed balance.** Its $2,000 drawdown follows closed trades, not open ones, and stops at the starting balance. Funded payouts need a best day of 40% of profit or less.

**Signature adds a daily limit only once funded.** In the evaluation there's none; funded accounts pause the day at $1,000 and cap the best day at 35% for a payout.

**The Zero accounts measure consistency against the target**: no single day above 40% of it to pass.

**Lots of room on time.** The forex accounts allow 60 days without trading; the futures accounts close after 7.
