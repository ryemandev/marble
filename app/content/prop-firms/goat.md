## What stands out

**The instant accounts trail on open profit.** Instant HERO's 50K allows $2,500 below your highest equity, stopping at the starting balance, with a 3% daily limit (from the day's start) that ends the account.

**Strict payout consistency.** Instant HERO and Instant GOAT need your best day to be 15% of profit or less, with five or six trading days.

**The evaluations are static.** The 1-Step 50K has a fixed $3,000 drawdown and a $5,000 target; the 2-Step GOAT a fixed $5,000 with targets of $4,000 then $3,000. Funded accounts need at least four trading days.

**News is restricted on the forex accounts.** Overnight and weekend holds are allowed.

**The futures accounts work differently.** Most trail at the end of the day, most close positions by 3:55 PM Central, and 7 days without trading closes an account.
