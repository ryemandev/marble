## What stands out

**The 1-Step Challenge has a best-day rule.** On the 50K: a $5,000 target, a $1,500 daily loss limit, and a $5,000 maximum loss that trails at the end of the day. No single day may be more than half of your profit, both to pass and once funded.

**The 2-Step Challenge has a static maximum loss.** $5,000 on the 50K, fixed from the start, with a $2,500 daily limit, targets of $5,000 then $2,500, and at least four trading days in each step.

**Breaking a daily limit ends the account** on every FTMO forex account, rather than pausing the day.

**Swing accounts keep weekends open.** The Standard account type restricts news and weekend holds once funded; the Swing type allows both, at lower leverage (1:30).

**FTMO Futures runs on its own rules.** Futures Growth and Futures Pro trail at the end of the day, close by 4:10 PM Eastern and start funded accounts at 2 contracts.
