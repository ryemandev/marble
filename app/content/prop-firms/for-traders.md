## What stands out

**Classic is a two-step with a static drawdown.** On the 50K: targets of $4,000 then $2,500, a $2,000 daily limit that ends the account, a fixed $4,000 drawdown, and three trading days per step.

**Fast trails on the closed balance.** Its $3,000 drawdown follows closed trades and stops at the starting balance; Fast Static keeps it fixed instead.

**Pay After Pass has a $1,000 target on the 50K**, with a 20% best-day rule for funded payouts.

**The instant accounts have the strictest payout rule**: no day above 15% of profit, and at least seven trading days.

**Leverage drops once funded** on the forex accounts, and news trading becomes restricted.

**The futures accounts** trail at the end of the day and stop at the starting balance. For Traders doesn't spell out a closing time for them, so check before holding late.
