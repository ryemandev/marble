## What stands out

**Alpha Direct is instant funding.** On the 50K: a 3% daily limit from the day's start that ends the account, $2,500 of drawdown trailing your closed balance until it reaches the start, and a best day of 15% of profit or less for a payout. No weekend holds.

**Alpha One is a single step** at three targets: 6%, 10% or 12%. The drawdown trails on closed balance; one trading day is enough to pass.

**Alpha Pro is two steps with two funded paths.** Bi-Weekly payouts need five trading days; On-Demand payouts need a best day of 40% of profit or less. The drawdown is static.

**Alpha Swing allows weekend holds** with lower leverage, and restricts news throughout.

**News is restricted once funded** on every Alpha account, and funded sizes are capped in lots.
