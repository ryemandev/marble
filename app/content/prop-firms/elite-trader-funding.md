## What stands out

**Seven very different accounts.** Elite Trader Funding sells live-trailing, end-of-day, static and overnight accounts, so the plan you pick decides most of your rules.

**1-Step trails on open profit.** On the 50K there's no daily loss limit, but the $2,000 drawdown follows your open profit as it happens. You need $3,000 and at least five trading days, with up to 8 contracts.

**End of Day adds a daily limit that ends the account.** Its $1,100 limit on the 50K isn't a pause for the day: breaking it fails the account.

**Diamond Hands and Direct to Funded allow overnight and weekend holds.** Most other plans close out by 3:59 PM Central.

**Fast Track is quick and tight.** The 10K accounts must pass within 10 days, with at least three trading days and no day worth more than 40% of profit.

Funded accounts left idle for 7 days are closed.
