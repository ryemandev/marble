## What stands out

**Every Bulenox account comes as Option 1 or Option 2.** Option 1 trails the drawdown intraday on open profit, with no daily loss limit and more contracts (7 on the 50K). Option 2 trails at the end of the day and adds a daily loss limit that pauses the day; on Fast Track and Momentum it also allows fewer contracts (4 instead of 7).

**Fast Track skips the evaluation.** It's funded from the start, with a strict payout rule: your best day must stay at or under 20% of profit.

**Momentum and Qualification are evaluations.** Both ask for $3,000 on the 50K inside 30 days. Momentum's payout rule caps the best day at 35%; Qualification's at 40%, with a slightly larger $2,500 drawdown.

**Close by 3:59 PM Central.** News trading is allowed. A funded Qualification account left for 7 days without trading is closed.
