## What stands out

**No daily loss limit.** None of TradeDay's three programmes has one; the drawdown is the only loss rule.

**Fast Pass is the shorter route.** On the 50K: $3,000 to make, at least three trading days, and no day worth more than 45% of your profit. The same 45% rule comes back for payouts. The drawdown trails at the end of the day and stops at the starting balance.

**Quick Pay asks for more days and steadier days.** Five trading days and a 30% best-day cap to pass. Quick Pay End of Day trails at the close during the evaluation, then intraday on open profit once funded; Quick Pay Intraday trails intraday from the start.

**Central-time close.** Every position must be flat by 3:50 PM Central, news trading is restricted, and an account idle for 30 days is closed.
