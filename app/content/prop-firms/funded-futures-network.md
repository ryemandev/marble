## What stands out

**New rules from 22 October 2026.** FFN changed the rules for accounts bought from that day. The tables below show the set sold now.

**MAX Standard has a daily limit and consistency at both ends.** On the 50K: a $1,250 daily loss limit that pauses the day, a $2,000 end-of-day trailing drawdown, at least five trading days, and no day above 40% of profit, both to pass and for a payout.

**STEADY has no daily loss limit.** It needs only two trading days, with a looser 52% best-day rule to pass and no consistency rule for payouts.

**Both can skip the evaluation.** The Skip the Eval versions start funded with the same funded-stage rules.

**News trading is restricted once funded.** Positions close by 4:50 PM Eastern, and 29 days without trading closes an account.
