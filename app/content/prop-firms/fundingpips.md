## What stands out

**Daily loss is measured from the day's start.** On the 2 Step Pro 50K it's 3% of the balance you start the day with, and breaking it ends the account. The maximum drawdown is a fixed $3,000.

**No minimum days on 2 Step Pro.** Both steps ask for $3,000 on the 50K, with up to 20 lots.

**Funded accounts are day trading only.** Evaluations allow overnight and weekend holds and news trading; once funded, positions close each day and news is restricted. Payouts need a best day of 35% of profit or less.

**FundingPips Zero is instant funding** with the strictest payout rule here: no day above 15% of profit, and at least seven trading days. Its drawdown trails intraday on open profit.

**Flex and Standard trade off room against targets.** 2 Step Flex allows $6,000 of static drawdown; 2 Step Standard $5,000 with a 5% daily limit.
