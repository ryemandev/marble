## What stands out

**The daily loss limit is measured from the day's start.** On High Stakes it's 4% of the balance you began the day with ($2,000 on a 50K account starting the day at $50,000), and breaking it ends the account.

**Drawdowns are static.** High Stakes allows $4,000 below the starting balance on the 50K, at every stage.

**Two High Stakes versions.** Classic asks for 8% in Step 1, New for 10%; both need 5% in Step 2 and at least three trading days per step.

**Funded accounts keep a target.** The funded High Stakes 50K still has a $5,000 profit target.

**Bootcamp is three steps** with small static drawdowns, and only adds a daily limit once funded.

**News is restricted on the forex accounts.** The futures Day Trade and Swing accounts allow it, trail at the end of the day, and close an account after 14 idle days.
