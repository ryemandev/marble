## What stands out

**The daily loss limit ends the evaluation.** On the 50K Gauntlet Mini it's $1,100, and breaking it fails the account rather than pausing the day.

**Passing needs consistency.** No single day may be more than 30% of your profit. The drawdown is $2,000, trailing your end-of-day balance and stopping at the starting balance.

**Size scales with your profit.** Both programmes start the 50K at 6 contracts and raise the limit as your balance grows. News trading is allowed, and positions close by 3:50 PM Central.

**Live accounts trail intraday.** Once live, the drawdown follows open profit as it happens.
