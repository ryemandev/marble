## What stands out

**One programme, five sizes.** The Trading Test comes in 25K, 50K, 75K, 100K and 150K. On the 50K you need $3,000, at least three trading days, and a best day of no more than 50% of your profit.

**No daily loss limit in the Test.** The only loss rule is the $2,000 drawdown, which trails your end-of-day balance and stops at the starting balance.

**The PRO account trails intraday.** Once funded, the drawdown follows open profit as it happens instead of waiting for the close, and news trading becomes restricted. That's a real change in how much room a winning trade leaves you.

**PRO+ adds a daily limit.** The PRO+ stage has a $1,000 daily loss limit and a tighter $1,250 drawdown on the 50K, with size capped at 2 contracts.

Positions must be closed by 4:55 PM Eastern at every stage.
