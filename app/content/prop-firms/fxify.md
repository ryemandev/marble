## What stands out

**Instant Funding has an 8% daily limit.** On the 50K that's $4,000 from the day's start, with a $4,000 drawdown that trails your closed balance and stops at the start. Weekend holds and news trading are restricted.

**Many FXIFY drawdowns trail on the closed balance.** One Phase, Two Phase and the crypto and Lightning accounts follow closed trades, then stop at the starting balance. Two Phase Static, Two Phase Pro and Three Phase keep a fixed drawdown instead.

**Minimum days vary.** One Phase, Two Phase and Three Phase need five trading days per step; Two Phase Pro three; Two Phase Static four.

**Lightning is a race.** Seven days to pass, with a 30% best-day rule.

**Futureify is FXIFY's futures side**: end-of-day trailing drawdown, a $1,000 daily limit that pauses the day, and positions closed by 4:59 PM Eastern.
