## What stands out

**Instant Funding Pro trails on open profit.** On the 50K: $3,000 of drawdown that follows your highest equity, a 3% daily limit from the day's start that ends the account, at least five trading days, and a best day of 20% of profit or less for a payout.

**Instant Funding Standard trails at the end of the day** instead, with a $2,500 drawdown and a 15% best-day rule.

**The Two Step accounts differ in drawdown type.** Two Step Standard and Elite are static ($4,000 and $6,000 on the 50K); Two Step Pro trails intraday on open profit.

**News is restricted on the forex accounts**, and three trading days are needed before most funded payouts.

**Aqua's futures accounts** trail at the end of the day, close by 4:10 PM Eastern and close after 7 idle days.
