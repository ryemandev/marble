## What stands out

**Express to Live has a very small, fixed drawdown.** On the 50K you have $650 of static drawdown, the same in every stage, against a $2,500 target. There's no daily loss limit, and it's day trading only.

**Fundamental and Premium trail at the end of the day.** Both have a $2,500 drawdown on the 50K that stops trailing at $50,100 once funded, and a $4,000 target. The funded stage adds a payout consistency rule: no day above 30% of profit.

**The difference is holding and days.** Fundamental closes out by 9:59 PM UTC and asks for at least 10 trading days once funded. Premium allows overnight and weekend holds and asks for 5.

**News trading is allowed** on every Phidias account.
