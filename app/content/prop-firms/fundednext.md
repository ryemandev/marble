## What stands out

**Stellar drawdowns are static.** On the 50K Stellar 1-Step you have a fixed $3,000 below the starting balance, a $1,500 daily loss limit that ends the account, a $5,000 target and at least two trading days.

**Stellar 2-Step is roomier but slower.** A $5,000 static drawdown and $2,500 daily limit on the 50K, targets of $4,000 then $2,500, and five trading days in each step. Funded payouts on 2-Step and Lite carry a 40% best-day rule.

**News trading changes once funded.** It's allowed in every evaluation and restricted on funded Stellar accounts.

**The futures accounts trail at the end of the day.** Futures Flex, Rapid Daily, Rapid Pro and the Direct account stop trailing at $50,100 on the 50K and close by 3:10 PM Central. Flex measures consistency against the target: no day above 40% of it.
