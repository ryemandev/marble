## What stands out

**Two kinds of drawdown, one choice.** Apex sells every size (25K to 150K) twice. The EOD accounts trail your end-of-day balance and add a daily loss limit ($1,000 on the 50K) that pauses the day. The intraday accounts have no daily limit, but the drawdown follows your open profit tick by tick, so a winner that turns around costs you room.

**30 days to pass.** The 50K evaluation asks for $3,000 inside 30 days, with up to 6 contracts and news trading allowed. Every position has to be flat by 4:59 PM Eastern.

**The funded account changes the rules.** In the Performance Account the drawdown stops trailing once its floor reaches $50,100 on the 50K, the daily limit rises as you build profit, and size starts at 4 contracts and scales. For a payout, no single day may be half or more of your profit since the last payout.

**Inactivity closes accounts.** A funded account with no trading for 30 days is closed, so a break needs planning.
