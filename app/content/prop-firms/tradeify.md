## What stands out

**Every Tradeify drawdown trails at the end of the day.** On the 50K accounts it's $2,000 below your highest close, and in the funded stage it stops trailing once the floor reaches $50,100.

**Growth is the quickest start.** One trading day minimum, a $3,000 target on the 50K and a $1,250 daily loss limit that pauses the day. The consistency rule only arrives with payouts: your best day must stay at or under 35% of profit.

**Select asks for more days and consistency up front.** Both Select accounts need three trading days and a best day of no more than 40% of profit to pass. Select Daily has a daily loss limit in the funded stage; Select Flex has none at any stage.

**Lightning Funded skips the evaluation**, with a tighter payout rule: no day above 20% of profit.

**Mind the clock and the calendar.** Positions must be closed by 4:45 PM Eastern, and an evaluation or funded account left idle for 7 days is closed.
