## What stands out

**Builder is the plan most traders start on.** On the 50K: a $3,000 target, a $1,000 daily loss limit that pauses the day, a $2,000 end-of-day trailing drawdown that stops at $50,100, and just one trading day minimum. For a payout, your best day must stay under half your profit.

**Rapid moves the consistency rule to the evaluation.** It has no daily loss limit but needs two trading days and a best day of no more than 50% of profit to pass. In the funded stage the drawdown switches to intraday trailing on open profit, and news trading becomes restricted. Rapid EOD keeps an end-of-day drawdown but asks for four days and a 30% best day.

**Studio accounts are micro-only**, with a small static drawdown: $500 on the 10K, trading micros only.

**Be flat by 4:10 PM Eastern**, and don't leave an account idle: 7 days without trading closes it.
