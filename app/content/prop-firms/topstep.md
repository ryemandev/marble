## What stands out

**The drawdown trails your end-of-day balance, then stops.** On the 50K Trading Combine you have $2,000 of room below your highest closing balance, and once that floor reaches the starting balance it stays there. Losses during the day count against it as they happen, so the room you start a session with is the room you have.

**There's no daily loss limit unless you set one.** The Combine has no firm daily limit, but you can choose one ($1,000 on the 50K). The Pro Account has a $1,000 limit that pauses the day, and Live accounts have one too.

**Passing needs consistency.** No single day may be more than 55% of your profit, and you need at least two trading days. In the funded stage you pick a path: the standard one, or the consistency path, where the best day must stay under 40% of profit for a payout.

**Be flat by 3:10 PM Central.** Every Topstep account closes positions before the session ends, and news trading is restricted. A funded account with no trades for 30 days is closed.
