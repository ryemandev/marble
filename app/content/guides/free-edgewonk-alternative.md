Marble is a free alternative to Edgewonk if you want your journal on your phone as well as your computer, or you trade prop accounts. Edgewonk lists no free plan: on 6 November 2026 it cost $35 a month or $197 a year. Marble is free forever, with every feature in it.

## What Edgewonk does well

Edgewonk has one plan with everything in it, and its users rate its support and its tools for finding what works in their trading. Like Marble, it leans on files more than live links. If you've built your review around its Edge Finder, that's a real reason to stay.

## What you'd give up

- **Edgewonk's own analysis tools.** Marble's Stats and Ask your journal work differently, so your routine would change.
- **Your history.** Bring your trades across from your platform's export. Your Edgewonk notes won't move on their own.

## What you'd get, for free

- **Your phone.** Open Marble in your browser anywhere, and add it to your home screen.
- **Prop firm rules.** Rules for 22 firms, and your fees against your payouts.
- **A place to talk.** The Floor is where traders share cards, ask questions and join crews.
- **Your whole record.** One zip with your data, a spreadsheet of your trades and every photo.

## Which one to pick

Pay for Edgewonk if its analysis tools are worth $197 a year to you. Pick Marble if you want a full journal at no cost. There's more in [free trading journals compared](/free-trading-journal).
