Marble is a free alternative to TradeZella if you're happy to import your trades from a file or log them by hand. TradeZella's pricing page lists no free plan, and on 6 November 2026 its plans ran from $35 to $99 a month. Marble is free forever, with every feature in it.

## What TradeZella does well

TradeZella is a mature product with live broker links, trade replay and a large community. Its users rate it highly on Trustpilot. If you want your trades to appear on their own, or you rely on replay, it does things Marble doesn't.

## What you'd give up

- **Live broker links.** Trades come into Marble from a file or by hand.
- **Trade replay.** Marble shows the trade's record, not a tick-by-tick replay.

## What you'd keep, for free

- **Every account in one place.** Add any account at any firm or broker. Your total P&L shows by day, week, month or any range you set.
- **Imports.** Drop the export from Tradovate, TopstepX, Rithmic, NinjaTrader, MetaTrader, cTrader or Interactive Brokers.
- **Prop firm rules.** Marble holds the rules for 22 firms, and shows your fees against your payouts.
- **Stats and review.** See what your mistakes cost, your costliest weekday and your day-by-day calendar.
- **Your whole record.** Export your data, a spreadsheet of your trades and every photo in one zip.

## Which one to pick

Pay for TradeZella if live sync and replay are worth $35 a month or more to you. Pick Marble if you want a full journal at no cost and don't mind bringing your trades in yourself. There's more on what each journal gives you for free in [free trading journals compared](/free-trading-journal).
