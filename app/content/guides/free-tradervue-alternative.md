Marble is a free alternative to Tradervue if you trade prop accounts or several accounts at once and are happy to bring your trades in from a file. Tradervue's paid plans started at $29.95 a month on 6 November 2026. Marble is free forever, with every feature in it.

## What Tradervue does well

Tradervue has been around for years, and traders use it for its deep reports and exit analysis. If you've built your review around those reports, that counts for a lot.

## What you'd give up

- **Live broker links.** Trades come into Marble from a file or by hand.
- **Years of habit.** Your old reports don't move across with you.

## What you'd get, for free

- **Net P&L in dollars.** Your total shows by day, week, month or any range, across every account.
- **Prop firm rules.** Rules for 22 firms, and your fees against your payouts.
- **A daily close.** Look over the day's trades, then close the day, even when you didn't trade.
- **Your whole record.** One zip with your data, a spreadsheet of your trades and every photo.

## Which one to pick

Stay with Tradervue if its reports are what you open every week. Pick Marble if you'd rather not pay and you want your prop accounts in one place. There's more in [free trading journals compared](/free-trading-journal).
